# Senior Platform Engineer

We are hiring a Senior Platform Engineer to design, build, and operate the
infrastructure our product teams ship on. You will own the paths from a commit
to production and make them fast, boring, and observable.

## What you will do

- Operate Kubernetes clusters across multiple regions
- Write and review Terraform modules for shared infrastructure
- Build CI/CD pipelines and shorten the feedback loop for product teams
- Instrument services for observability and lead incident response
- Partner with data teams on analytical workloads

## What we are looking for

- Deep familiarity with distributed systems and their failure modes
- Production Kubernetes and Docker
- Go or Rust for services; Python for tooling
- Infrastructure as code, and a preference for automation over runbooks
- Comfort with PostgreSQL, including query tuning

## Nice to have

- AWS Certified Solutions Architect certification
- Exposure to Kafka or another streaming platform
- A Bachelor's degree in Computer Science or equivalent practice
